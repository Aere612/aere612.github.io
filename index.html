ULUDOTT
We founded a game development student club at university, named Uludağ Üniversitesi Dijital Oyun Tasarım Topluluğu. (Uludag University Digital Game Development Club). We first teached new comers how to develop games, and than put them in project groups where they can together develop new ones. I teached C# and Unity lessons there personally as well. Also managing project groups and seeing student creating new games was an amazing experience. At the second year of the club we made an national game jam, ULUJAM.
My First Work Life

Over a total of two years of internship and employment, I developed more than 15 games, including various match-3 titles and even a Zuma-style game. During this time, I gained hands-on experience with SDK integration, Git, version control systems, and working within a professional team environment under deadlines. I also learned how to prepare and present quarterly reports to executives.
